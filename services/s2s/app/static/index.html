A two-person voice call where each side speaks their own Indian language and hears the other person translated, with live captions.

  • Share the room link with the other person after you join.
  • Pause briefly after each sentence; the translation starts about 1.5 s later.
  • Say “Hey Mind …” to ask the assistant something privately: only you hear the answer, and the others don't hear the question.
  • In the same room as the other person? Use headphones, or each mic picks up the other side.
